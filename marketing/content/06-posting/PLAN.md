## 3. The 72h check (per video)
Read in TikTok Studio, per-video analytics: views, average watch time, full-watch %, retention curve,
shares, saves. Write the numbers into tracker.csv and screenshot into 04-stats-72h/<id>/.

THE THRESHOLDS BELOW ARE MY HEURISTICS. TikTok publishes none. Adjust after 5 videos of real data.

| Observed at 72h | Diagnosis | Action |
|---|---|---|
| views < 100 | Not distributed. Not a hook problem | No recut. Check caption keyword, account restrictions, AI label. Re-check at 7 days |
| views >= 100 and avg watch < 5 s (or retention < 50% at 3 s) | Hook fails | Recut hook: new first 3 s (text, voice, opening frame), different hook formula |
| good first 3 s, full-watch < 15% | Body too slow | Recut shorter (<= 20 s), faster pacing |
| good watch, shares + saves < 1% of views | Nothing to share | Recut ending: add a save/share reason |
| views >= 3x account median, or full-watch >= 30% | Winner | No recut. Make a follow-up on the same angle |

Recut rules: max 2 recuts per video, then retire it. A recut must differ in hook line, voice and the
first 3 s of footage, not just a new caption (near-duplicate posting from one account risks
suppression). Post the recut >= 7 days after the original. Keep the original up unless it breaks a rule.
