# Copy rules (use for every caption, hook, voice line, endcard)

## The message
You already have a farm, or a patch of land, or a dream of one. MyTerra is where you put it, so you can see it and start.
The app follows YOU. It never "designs your farm" or tells you how your land should look.

## Say this / not this
| Not this | This |
|---|---|
| "I let an app design my farm" | "Tell the app what you have" |
| "The app tells me what to do" | "It walks you through, one small step at a time" |
| "Total beginner" (shame) | "No experience needed. Beginners welcome" |
| "My farm" (it is a demo) | "A demo farm, so you can see how it works. Yours will look like yours" |
| "Follow so you don't miss it" | "Follow along with me" |

## Psychology (honest, no pressure)
1. Identity: the viewer is already the farmer. Use "your land", "your farm", "you".
2. Fear down: name the worry (no idea where to start, can't hold it all in your head) and answer it.
3. Small wins: "three small jobs", "ten minutes", "one tap".
4. Seeing it: people commit when they can picture it (the growth preview, the map).
5. Belonging: "follow along with me", "we plant", "our first harvest".
6. Open loop: end every part with what happens next.
Never: fake scarcity, guilt, countdown pressure, income claims, or presenting the demo as real results.

## Every caption must have
- Keyword first line (farming app / grow your own food).
- Demo-farm line.
- Website: myterra.farm (TikTok does not make links clickable, so it is written out).
- "Try it free for 7 days" (landing page: 7-day free trial, no card).
- Next-part teaser. Seven hashtags.
