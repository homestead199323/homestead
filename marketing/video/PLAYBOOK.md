# MyTerra short-video playbook

Every MyTerra video is made to spread, not just to inform. The video
generator (`marketing/video/`) is built around these rules. Check each new
episode against the checklist at the bottom before it is posted.

Research basis (Oct 2026): TikTok confirms that strong signals such as
finishing a video outweigh weak ones, and that follower count is not a direct
ranking factor. Shares, saves, rewatches and profile visits are what spread a
video. Sources are at the end.

## 1. The first 3 seconds (three hook layers that agree)
- **Visual:** frame 1 is already the payoff, in motion. Use the finished 3D
  farm panning or the jobs pop-up opening. Never a logo, a fade-in or a
  welcome screen.
- **Text on frame 1:** a white box near the top that says what the voice says,
  plus a series pill ("PART 1"). Many people watch muted, so the text may be
  the real hook.
- **Voice:** 8–15 words, with the keyword ("farming app", "grow food",
  "what to plant") spoken inside the first 3 seconds.
- Hook formulas, in order of preference:
  - curiosity gap: "I let a farming app design my entire farm"
  - contrarian: "You don't need a garden to grow 40 kg of food"
  - callout: "If you've killed every plant you've owned…"
  - stakes: "Don't plant tomatoes before you see this"
  - listicle: "3 things this app knew about my soil"
- Banned in the first 5 seconds: context, throat-clearing, "Hi guys", app
  name intros.

## 2. Body: real app, real movement
- Real screens only, recorded frame by frame from the live app: real taps,
  typing, scrolling, map drags, zooms, pop-ups. No mock-ups or stills.
- Something changes every 1–2 seconds: a tap, scroll, cut or zoom. Slow setup
  steps are speed-ramped (1.8–2.5×). Payoff moments play at real speed.
- A finger dot and ripple on every tap, a small punch-in zoom on each tap, and
  a soft "pop" sound.
- Story shape: setup ("total beginner") → tension ("will it work?") → payoff
  (the farm appears, the jobs list, the harvest).
- Captions: 1–3 words at a time, uppercase, current word highlighted yellow,
  placed at about 70% of the screen height, clear of TikTok's buttons on the
  right and its text at the bottom.

## 3. Ending: loop and cliffhanger
- The last frame matches the first frame, so the video loops into a rewatch.
  Rewatches are a strong signal.
- Each episode ends with an open loop: "Tomorrow I plant the first seeds. Part
  two." Series episodes get noticeably more profile visits, and profile visits
  turn into follows.
- No "link in bio" begging in the video. The profile does that job.

## 4. Length and rhythm
- Build and feature episodes run 20–35 seconds. A "What to plant this month"
  explainer can run up to 50 seconds.
- Post 3–4 times a week (Tuesday to Thursday is best), 9am–12pm or around 3pm
  in the target audience's time zone (UK/EU first).
- Keep a fixed weekly schedule and a consistent cover style ("PART N" pill and
  the same hook box).

## 5. Search (TikTok is a search engine too)
- Put the keyword in four places: spoken in the first 3 seconds, on screen in
  frame 1, at the start of the post caption, and in a pinned comment (under
  80 characters).
- Hashtags: 1–2 broad (#gardening #homestead), 2–3 niche (#growyourownfood
  #allotment #beginnergardener), and 1–2 specific (#raisedbeds #ukgarden).
- Keep the account on one topic. Every video is about growing food with the
  app.

## 6. Honesty and labelling
- Only show features that exist in the live app, with true numbers from the
  app.
- The voice is AI (Kokoro TTS). Turn on TikTok's AI-generated content label
  whenever its rules require it. One industry source reports lower engagement
  for AI-labelled posts, so a human voice-over is the upgrade path if
  performance stalls.
- No fake reviews, no fake user counts, no "I made £X" claims the app can't
  back up.

## 7. Series plan
**"Building a farm from zero"** (one continuous farm, one feature per part):
1. The app designs the farm (onboarding → 3D map → jobs)
2. Sowing the first seeds, then the growth preview: 3 months in 5 seconds
3. The chickens move in (livestock, egg jobs)
4. First harvest goes into the pantry
5. Selling the surplus (Pantry → Sell → Financials)
6. Frost warning night (weather moves jobs)
7. Seed trays in the nursery
8. Asking the farm AI

**Evergreen feature shorts** (rotate crops and regions automatically):
"What to plant in [month] in [country]", "[Crop] from seed to harvest in 20
seconds", "Balcony vs backyard vs farm", "How much food does 4×8 m grow",
"Day vs night on my farm" (loop bait).

## Pre-post checklist
- [ ] Frame 1 shows the payoff, in motion
- [ ] Hook text on frame 1 matches the voice line
- [ ] Keyword spoken within the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Something changes at least every 2 seconds
- [ ] Every tap has a finger mark and a sound
- [ ] Captions readable and clear of TikTok's buttons and text
- [ ] Ends on the opening frame (loop) with a cliffhanger line
- [ ] 20–35 seconds
- [ ] Only real, live features; numbers match the app
- [ ] Post caption starts with the keyword; pinned comment written; hashtags set
- [ ] AI label decision made
