# MyTerra UX/UI audit — 2026-09-28

**Scope:** full walkthrough as the target user (millennial, zero growing experience), on the
production build at phone (390×844, 360×740), tablet and desktop (1440×900) sizes, light and dark.
**Personas:** (A) London backyard 8 × 5 m, total beginner, 15 min/day, 2 people, owns nothing;
(B) Amsterdam balcony 4 × 1.5 m, part-covered, 3–5 h sun, 5 min/day; (C) skips setup;
(D) established farm with hens and bees (test fixture). Trial states: day 1, last day, expired.
Auth flows (sign-up, wrong password, reset request, reset link) run against mocked Supabase
responses so no production account was created.

Status: **Fixed** = shipped in the UX pass commit (see `LAUNCH_PLAN.md`, "UX pass").

## Blockers — sign-up and trust

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Every "Start free / Build my growing space" link opened `/app` in **"Welcome back — sign in"** mode; new visitors had to find a small "Sign up free" link. | Fixed: CTAs go to `/app?signup`; the form has "Create account / Sign in" tabs and trial copy ("Start your 7-day free trial · No card needed"). |
| 2 | No **"Forgot password?"** — a locked-out user had no way back in. | Fixed: reset email + "Choose a new password" screen (PASSWORD_RECOVERY), landing page forwards auth links to `/app`. |
| 3 | With email confirmation on, supabase-js 2.106 returns `user: null` for sign-up, so the form hung on **"Please wait…"** forever. | Fixed: any sign-up without a session shows "check your email". |
| 4 | Menu and Settings said **"Free plan"** for everyone (there is no free plan). | Fixed: real plan ("Pro trial · 6 days left", "Basic plan", "Trial ended · read-only") with a "Plans" button. |
| 5 | Enter did not submit sign-in / sign-up; Supabase error text shown raw. | Fixed: Enter submits; errors say what to do next; show/hide password. |

## High — the first week lied to beginners

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---------|--------|
| 6 | Onboarding marked picks as **already sown** today; the "Sow" job vanished after 3 days; crops showed "Sown" and a harvest countdown for seeds never bought. | Fixed: picks wait as "Ready to sow"; the sow job stays until done; doing it (task, map popup, walk, or "I sowed it today") starts the clock that day. |

| 8 | Day 1 said "Sow radish, lettuce, strawberry" to someone who owns **nothing**. | Fixed: starter kit (seeds/plants with counts, bed or planters, compost litres, trowel + can) on the plan step and as a checklist on Home; sowing moves to day 2. |
| 9 | Starter bed ignored time: 40 m² garden at 15 min/day → a **10 m², 2.6 m wide** bed (middle unreachable) with 60 plants. | Fixed: bed sized by time (15 min → 1.2 × 2 m), 1.2 m wide, plants laid out in real rows with the "Plant a crop" planner (24 radish, 8 lettuce, 3 strawberry). |
| 10 | "Here's your starter map" showed **no map**. | Fixed: live read-only 3D preview of the generated space. |
| 11 | Same job, two labels: Home "Worth a look" vs Tasks red **"Needs attention — miss the window and plants bolt, fail or spoil"** for sowing radish. | Fixed: calm "Do today" (red/orange only when a harvest is ready), time estimate on every job (~10 min). |
| 12 | Ticking a job off gave **no feedback**; eggs/milk ticked on the Tasks screen never reached the pantry. | Fixed: confirmation toast with "Next: thin in 14 days" and Undo; Tasks uses the same completion as the map popup. |
| 13 | Assistant button covered onboarding buttons and the full-screen map's zoom controls; "full screen" still showed the tab bar. | Fixed: hidden during onboarding and full-screen map. |
| 14 | The 3D map kept **animating behind onboarding** (battery/CPU on phones). | Fixed: nothing renders under onboarding. |

## Open — not changed in this pass

- **Navigation** is still Farm / Tasks / Crops / Animals / More. The brief's Today / My Space / Plan / Learn / Progress (Stage 5) would give balcony users a nav that isn't farm- and animal-first.
- **Morning walk intro** asks "Where are you?" and "Start from" before the first walk. That's a lot of choices for someone who just joined.
- **Map controls** are icon-only (calendar icon = growth preview), with labels only for screen readers.
- **Landing page** is ~14,700 px tall on a phone; not re-audited here beyond the CTA links.
- **Import Backup** replaces all data without a confirmation step.
- **No account deletion or password change** in Settings (GDPR / App Store requirement).
- The "+ Plant Crop" header button is still shown when there is no bed yet.

## Not verifiable from here (check in the Supabase dashboard)

- **Emails reach real users:** Auth → SMTP. Supabase's built-in sender only delivers to team members
  and is heavily rate-limited; confirmation and reset emails need a custom SMTP provider.
- **Redirect allow-list** includes `https://myterra.farm/app` and `https://myterra-sigma.vercel.app/app`
  (Auth → URL Configuration). If not, links fall back to the Site URL; the landing page now forwards
  them to `/app`, so both work, but the allow-list is cleaner.
- Real-device frame rate of the 3D preview on low-end phones.
